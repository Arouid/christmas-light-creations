## Negative keywords (account level, so Performance Max honors them)

In December most "christmas lights pearland" searches are people looking for **displays to visit**, not installers. Add as phrase negatives: `light show`, `light display`, `displays near me`, `drive thru`, `drive through`, `festival of lights`, `zoo lights`, `where to see`, `best neighborhoods`, `tour`, `events`, `tree lighting`. Shoppers and DIY: `for sale`, `buy`, `walmart`, `home depot`, `amazon`, `lowes`, `how to`, `diy`, `clips`, `extension cord`. Work seekers: `jobs`, `hiring`, `job`, `salary`. Out of scope: `tile roof` is fine to leave; `permanent christmas lights installation` (we don't install permanent systems; keep "not working" and "failed" searches).

Check **Insights → Search terms** each Monday (the Home → Google Ads panel shows the top 5) and add new display or job searches as they appear.
