# Season plan 2026: mid-October to takedowns

Made 2026-10-14 with the marketing plugin's campaign-plan, competitive-brief and brand-review methods, from what the site, the staff app and the docs say today. Drafts and a plan only: **nothing is posted, sent, published to a profile or changed in Google Ads by Claude.** The owner posts and changes; staff send from the app; Claude drafts, renders and checks when asked.

Material: [brand voice](brand-voice.md) · [competitors](competitors-2026-10.md) · [Google profile posts](google-business-posts.md) · [social posts](social-posts.md) · [Google Ads copy](google-ads-copy.md) · [rebook email](rebook-email.md).

## In short

- **The season has four messages, set by the calendar the website already follows** (`urgency` in content.js): *install by Oct 31 and save 10%* (Oct 15 to 31), *dates fill fast* (November), *installing now* (Nov 30 to Dec 15), *thank you, takedowns Jan 3 to 13, booking 2027* (after Dec 15). Every post, ad and email swaps on those dates.
- **Reviews are the job that matters most.** Rivals on the Pearland lists show 42 to 139 reviews; we show 3. The automatic ask after every install and the ★ card on the account page are live. Staff's part is small and daily: mark installs **Install Completed** the same day, send the ⭐ texts the day they appear, tap **They reviewed**. Target: about 40 by the end of January.
- **Returning customers first.** One email in the week of Oct 15 pointing to the one-tap "Yes, put my lights back up" (live), one follow-up around Nov 9 to those who didn't answer, then stop.
- **Google Ads: three copy swaps** (Oct 15, Nov 1, Nov 30), a Monday look at search terms, and the "light display" searches blocked before December, when they flood in.
- **About 1½ hours a week for the owner, about 1 hour a week plus a few minutes a day for staff.** Claude drafts the next swap and checks the numbers when asked.

## Returning customers

- **The button**: on `/account/`, a returning customer who isn't on this season's list sees "Want your lights back up this season? One tap and you're on the list" with week chips and a note, and **Yes, put my lights back up**. Staff get a notification and an email; the season goes to Confirmed - Needs to be Scheduled. `rebookMe` answered 401 (deployed) to an anonymous probe on 2026-10-14.
- **The email**: [rebook-email.md](rebook-email.md), two versions, two sends per person at most.
- **Who said yes**: Season → Installs → ✨ Plan with Claude places them; text the date.
- **The ★ card**: past customers see the review card on their account until they reviewed. Rebook and review are separate on purpose: the review never rides on the booking.

| ★ card on `/account/` | Every past customer sees "Enjoying your lights?" with the review button until **They reviewed** | Live (`myAccount` deployed) |
| Missing-reviews case with Google (4-0202000041886) | Asks Google to restore the 2016 to 2021 reviews lost in the 2024 suspension | Open; the Friday 2026-10-16 reminder checks it |

The levers, all inside Google's rules (same words to everyone, nothing offered, one ask and one reminder):

1. **Mark Install Completed the day of the install.** The clock starts there; a status set a week late is a week of asks lost.
2. **Send the ⭐ texts the same day.** Customers who prefer texts only get the ask when a person sends it.
3. **Fill in missing emails and phone numbers.** No contact, no ask.
4. **Tap They reviewed** when a new review appears, so nobody is reminded after reviewing.
5. **Reply to every review** within a few days (owner).
6. **At about 10 reviews**, the rating and count can go near the hero (TODO N, true numbers only). Not before.

Never: a discount, gift or draw for a review; asking only the happy ones; asking family (Lacie's reviews were left out of the Google case for that reason); writing a review for anyone; a third message.
