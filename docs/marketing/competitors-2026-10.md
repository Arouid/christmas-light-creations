## What this means for us

| | Finding | So we |
|---|---|---|
| Biggest threat | **Reviews.** The directory's top names show 42 to 139 reviews; Houston Xmas Lighting puts its 11 on its own page; we show 3 on Google and none on the directory. Reviews decide the map pack and what the AI assistants repeat. | Make the review ask the season's first job (season plan, "Reviews: 3 to 40"). |
| Not unique | **Lifetime warranty**: Lone Star Lights and Houston Xmas Lighting both claim one. "Installation, takedown, storage included" and "only our own lights" are common too. | Never say "only we". Lead with the details that are ours: worn-out lights replaced **new**, **15 years without charging a cent**, free service calls with no limit. |
| Ours alone (on 10/14) | Nobody else shows: a **free designer on a photo of your own house** (rivals make the mock-up for you), **Pay in 3**, a **price beat** of 10% on a written quote, a **customer account and app**, a **cost guide** that explains the price. | These are the ad headlines and the posts. |
| Real difference | Takedown: Lone Star Lights says lights are down **by the end of January**; ours are down **by January 13th**. | "Lights down by January 13" headline. Say ours, never theirs. |
| Price anchors | $200 to $400 (Thumbtack's handyman figure), from $500 (Lone Star), $500 to $2,000 (Houston Xmas Lighting), $1,500 to $6,000+ (Bring the Bright). We publish none (owner's rule). | Keep it: the cost guide says why, the price beat answers comparisons, Pay in 3 answers "too much at once". |
| Urgency | Rivals use discount codes and pop-ups ("Wait! Before You Go! Get $50 Off Now!"). | Ours stays calendar-true: the Oct 31 discount, then the staff's real "how full we are" line, then "Installing now". |
| Response time | Lone Star: within 24 hours; Houston Xmas Lighting: 24/7 emergency. Ours: usually the same day locally, 48 hours at most in peak weeks. | Comparable, so no "fastest" claim; say ours as written. |
| Being found | Thumbtack and the directory list rivals; AI search answers named Thumbtack, HomeAdvisor, Alignable and Blingle, not us. | Already in the SEO report (TODO #79): claim the directory listing, Thumbtack, Angi, Nextdoor. Not repeated here. |
