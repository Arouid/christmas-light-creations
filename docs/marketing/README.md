## Rules every draft follows

- Every claim is on the website today (`src/data/content.js`, `src/data/areas.js`); when the site changes, these change.
- No dollar prices beyond the published offers; no star rating until about 10 reviews; no made-up reviews, numbers or stories; reviews quoted as written.
- Photos: only the owner's installs from `public/images/gallery/`, captioned with their `place`; nothing AI-generated or retouched presented as our work.
- Stock material: `assets-source/stock/envato/README.md`.
- No phone number in Google post text; no "#1" or "best"; no competitor named; no tree-wrapping or gift-wrapping pitch.
