## What works, keep it

- Two fonts (Fraunces, Inter) and the theme colors; no sideways scroll on any page at 375 px.
- Every gallery photo's description names its neighborhood and town; decorative images are silent to screen readers.
- Every form field has a visible label, required fields are marked, phones get the right keyboard and autofill.
- The 404 is on-brand with two ways forward; the account's nothing-found screen offers three ways out.
- The designer says plainly that it doesn't work with a screen reader and gives the number to call.

## Watch (for whoever changes these pages)

- A new estimate button or form on a public page needs `data-bar-hide`, so the bottom bar steps aside.
- The area-page header in `scripts/build-areas.mjs` copies `Header.jsx`: change both.
