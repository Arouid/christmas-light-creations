Christmas Light Creations - logo concepts saved as icons, 2026-10-10

These are the icon-only marks from the comparison pages, kept for reference. The live site uses the
neon cottage (see ../README.txt); nothing here is on the site.

Each folder has the SVG (lettering converted to outlines, no fonts needed) and PNG exports on a
transparent background at 512, 192, 180 (apple-touch), 48, 32 and 16 px.

Round 1 (first comparison page)
  a-camel-crest       camel head with Santa hat in a night roundel with a gold ring
  b-bulb              one lit C9 bulb on a wire; -on-night (white wire) and -on-white (navy wire)
  c-roofline          house with lights along the roof and a star, rounded night tile
  d-clc-monogram      CLC in Fraunces over a string of three bulbs, rounded night tile
  e-light-ring        ring of red and gold bulbs around CLC, night roundel
  r-camel-redraw      the old camel badge redrawn as clean vector shapes

Shortlist (2012-blend page, the owner's keepers)
  m1-neon-cottage     the 2012 cottage, neon glow, on the 2012 blue sky, rounded tile
  m2-cottage-badge    cottage and snowy pine in a gold-ringed round badge
  m3-sign             wooden signpost reading CLC with holly, pine behind (transparent)
  m5-neon-lines       the cottage as glowing outlines on the night sky, rounded tile

The ten "house style" variations (H1-H10) were not saved; ask if you want them.
Tiles and roundels carry their own night-blue background. The m1/m2/m5 glow is an SVG blur filter.
